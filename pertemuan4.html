1. The Nature of Communication Media

Learning media serves as an instructional Channel to deliver messages from sender (Teacher) to receiver (Student), reducing verbalism and communication barriers.

2. Cognitive Load Theory

Human working memory has limited capacity. Media selection must avoid cognitive bottlenecks:

  • Modality Principle: Balance visual and auditory signals simultaneously.
  • Redundancy Principle: Avoid reading aloud identical long text already visible on screen.

Evaluation Framework (Gary J. Anglin)

The ACTION Framework

6 Primary dimensions to evaluate the feasibility of learning media in institutional settings.

A

Access

How easily accessible is the media for all learners and instructors without licensing or geographic barriers?

C

Cost

Cost-effectiveness of creation, purchasing, and long-term maintenance relative to user reach.

T

Technology

Availability of power, internet connectivity, hardware support, and technical infrastructure stability.

I

Interactivity

The degree to which media fosters critical thinking, active participation, and immediate feedback.

O

Organization

Institutional leadership support, policy alignment, and organizational culture readiness.

N

Novelty

Does the novelty factor boost learning motivation or create unnecessary cognitive distraction?
